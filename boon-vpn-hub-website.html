NEED TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Which devices are supported?

The service is advertised for routers, mobile phones, Smart TVs, MacBooks and PCs. Exact compatibility depends on the VPN protocol, app and configuration supplied.

Are free VPN files available?

The channel advertises free files as well as paid configurations. Contact the admin for current availability and the official download source.

Can I get a 60-day or 90-day plan?

Longer durations can be arranged according to the supplied price list. Ask the admin for a confirmed quote before paying.

Is unlimited data really unlimited?

The seller lists an unlimited option. Ask for written confirmation of any fair-use policy, speed limits, traffic caps and renewal conditions before purchasing.

Does a VPN guarantee no logs or complete anonymity?

No VPN can promise complete anonymity simply by using a tunnel. Review the provider's actual logging policy, privacy terms and jurisdiction before relying on those claims.

How do I get support or payment details?

Contact the admin through the Telegram link on this website. Confirm the recipient and amount independently before making a payment.
